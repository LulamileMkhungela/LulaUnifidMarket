End-to-end flow

From advert to payout without trusting screenshots

1

Investigate

Seller uploads VIN/title/deed documents. TrustShield verifies identity, ownership, finance, stolen/title status, and inspection evidence.

2

Confirm funds

Buyer authorises a temporary provider check. Seller sees only “sufficient for this deal”, not full balance or bank statements.

3

Lock terms

Negotiation freezes description, price, VIN/title fields, known defects, and evidence hashes. Changes require buyer-approved addendum.

4

Escrow

Buyer funds a regulated escrow/trust partner. Lula orchestrates milestones and evidence; funds stay safeguarded by the partner.

5

Handover

Verifier checks final VIN/title/condition/transfer documents. Buyer accepts or opens dispute.

6

Release

Escrow pays seller only when conditions are met. If red flags appear, release pauses or refunds.

Aggressive anti-scam rules

If it changes after trust is built, it cannot silently continue.

  • VIN/title/deed tamper: remove listing immediately.
  • Description edit after negotiation: block unless buyer accepts addendum.
  • Fake proof of payment: ignored; only provider confirmation or escrow funding counts.
  • Bank-detail substitution: freeze and verify through trusted partner registry.
  • New stolen/ownership red flag: remove listing and freeze release.
